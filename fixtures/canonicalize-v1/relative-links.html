Bridging assets to Base

Moving assets to Base means locking them on the settlement layer and minting a representation here. The canonical bridge is the one the chain itself recognises; a third-party bridge is a separate trust assumption you are choosing to take on.

Read deploying a contract first if you have not already, and keep the address reference open while you work. The documentation source is public.

Withdrawals back to the settlement layer are subject to a challenge window. Budget for it: the funds are not lost, but they are not spendable there either until it elapses.
